Cookies & Browser Storage

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This website does not use cookies. There is no cookie banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to — no cookie is written to your browser by any page here.

Most sites that show you a cookie banner are asking permission to track you for advertising or analytics. We do neither, so this page exists to say plainly what we do store, rather than to ask you to click "Accept".

What we do not do

  • No cookies of any kind — not first-party, not third-party.
  • No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no advertising network, no heatmap or session-recording tool.
  • No tracking across other websites, and no advertising profile.
  • No behavioural profiling of students. Most of our students are minors and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 prohibits tracking and targeted advertising directed at children. Our systems contain no such capability.

If you only browse the public website

Nothing is stored on your device. You can read about our programmes, campuses and results, and submit an admission enquiry, without anything being written to your browser.

Two things still reach a third party, and we would rather say so:

  • Google Fonts — our pages load typefaces from Google's font servers, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. Nothing else is sent, and no cookie is set by us as a result.
  • Hostinger — our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, as any web host does.

If you are College staff signing in to a portal

The administration portals store a small number of values in your browser's local storage. Local storage is not a cookie: it is never attached to requests automatically, it is not readable by other websites, and it is not used for tracking. Every one of these is strictly necessary for the portal to function.

NameWhat it is forHow long it lasts
auth_token Proves you are signed in, so each screen does not ask for your password again Until it expires or you sign out
refresh_token Renews your session quietly, so a shift is not interrupted by repeated sign-ins Until it expires or you sign out
jc_sync_journal Notes whether the figures on screen are current, so stale data is not mistaken for fresh Until cleared
portal_theme_mode Remembers whether you chose light or dark appearance Until cleared
session_end_reason Lets the sign-in screen explain why you were signed out, instead of just returning you there Cleared when you close the tab

Why we do not ask for consent

Consent is required for storage that is not strictly necessary — tracking, analytics and advertising. Everything listed above is required for the portal to work at all: without the session values you cannot stay signed in. A banner asking you to accept them would be asking a question with only one possible answer.

If we ever add analytics or anything that is not strictly necessary, we will ask first, and this page will change before it happens.

Removing them

Signing out clears the session values. Clearing your browser's site data for inspirehnk.org removes all of them, and signs you out. You may also block storage for this site in your browser settings — the public website will work normally, but the staff portals will not be able to keep you signed in.
